# Retail Inventory & Store Operations Analysis

## Project Overview

This project analyzes retail inventory and store transaction data to understand inventory movement, sales activity, stock availability, and operational performance.

The project is designed around a realistic business scenario where a retail operations team needs reliable data before making inventory and operational decisions.

## Business Problem

A retail company is experiencing challenges in understanding product demand and inventory movement. Some products may be selling quickly and experiencing stock availability issues, while other products may remain in inventory for longer periods.

The objective of this project is to prepare and analyze the data to identify operational patterns, inventory issues, and potential business opportunities.

## Project Objectives

* Assess the quality of the raw dataset
* Identify and document data-quality issues
* Clean and standardize the dataset
* Validate the cleaned data
* Analyze sales and inventory performance
* Identify meaningful business insights
* Develop data visualizations and recommendations
